# Quarterly Access Review Plan

## Campaign

**Name:** Q3 Finance Reporting Access Review  
**Scope:** Finance Reporting application groups and roles  
**Reviewer:** Application owner, with manager consultation  
**Frequency:** Quarterly  
**Decision deadline:** 14 calendar days

## Objectives

- Confirm continuing business need
- Identify inactive and orphaned accounts
- Remove access retained after role changes
- Review privileged and exception access
- Record evidence for audit and remediation

## Review data

Each record should include:

- Identity and employee status
- Department and manager
- Target account
- Role or entitlement
- Assignment source
- Last successful sign-in when permitted
- Approval and expiration
- Risk or SoD flag

## Decision options

| Decision | Use when | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Approve | Access remains required | Business justification |
| Revoke | Access is no longer required | Target removal confirmation |
| Investigate | Ownership or need is unclear | Assigned owner and due date |
| Delegate | Correct reviewer is another owner | Named delegate |
| Time-limit | Temporary need remains | Expiration and owner |

## Escalation

- Day 7: reminder to reviewer
- Day 12: escalation to application owner
- Day 14: apply the documented non-response policy
- Immediate: security escalation for active leavers, orphaned privileged accounts, or suspicious activity

## Completion evidence

- Campaign scope and reviewer list
- Decisions and justifications
- Revocation transactions
- Target-system validation
- Exceptions and expiration dates
- Final summary and unresolved risks
