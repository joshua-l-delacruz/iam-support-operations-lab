# SailPoint Simulation - Certification Campaign

## Campaign design

**Campaign:** Quarterly Store and Finance Access Certification  
**Reviewers:** Managers for standard access; application owners for sensitive roles  
**Scope:** Active workforce and contractors with Store or Finance entitlements

## Reviewer guidance

Reviewers should consider:

- Current employment and department
- Continuing business need
- Assignment source and approval
- Last activity when available
- Sensitive, privileged, or conflicting access
- Contractor expiration
- Unowned or unmatched accounts

## Remediation controls

- High-risk revocations are prioritized.
- Every removal requires target confirmation.
- Failed revocations create incidents.
- Exceptions require an owner and expiration.
- Campaign completion does not hide unresolved remediation.

## Scope statement

This document models certification concepts and support evidence. It does not represent a production SailPoint campaign.
